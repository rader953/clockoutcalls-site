How it works

  1. Keep your number

    Calls you don't pick up, and calls after hours, forward to us through your phone carrier's call forwarding. Nothing changes for your customers.

  2. We pick up

    An AI assistant answers, tells the caller it's an AI, takes their name, address and the problem, and books an open slot on your calendar.

  3. You get a text

    A short summary hits your phone within minutes. Emergencies like gas smells or flooding get transferred straight to your cell.
